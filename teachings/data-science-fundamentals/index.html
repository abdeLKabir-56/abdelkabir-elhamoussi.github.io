About the Training

I taught students the basics of web development and helped them apply what they learned through practical exercises and projects.

Topics Covered

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • PHP
  • Web development fundamentals
  • Basic computer architecture

My Role

During this experience, I:

  • Taught frontend development basics with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Introduced students to backend development with PHP.
  • Explained basic computer architecture and web development concepts.
  • Guided students through practical exercises and projects.
  • Helped students understand and apply the concepts covered during the sessions.

What I Learned

This experience helped me improve my communication and teaching skills, especially when explaining technical concepts to people with different levels of experience.

It also gave me a different perspective on software development by requiring me to explain concepts clearly and focus on the practical side of what students were learning.
